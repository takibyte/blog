## The plan

Regarding how I see my path into becoming a cybersecurity professional, my plan is essentially to learn and practice as much as I can, on the things relevant to an entry-level position. From there I naturally will want to keep learning and progressing while on the job.

### Entry point and covering theory with certs

From what I understand, one of the most accessible entry points into cybersecurity is the SOC Analyst Level 1 (or equivalent) position. For this I'll need to develop and show a good basic IT proficiency, as well as networking fundamentals, and security fundamentals. After looking around online and doing some research, the CompTIA Network+ and Security+ certifications seem to be well suited to making sure I know (and can prove I know) these fundamentals from a theory perspective — so getting these certs should be a priority.

### Strategy for developing practical abilities

The cybersecurity profession is not just about theory however, I need to get hands-on experience with a variety of systems, tools, and scenarios, and I need to be able to show that experience. That is where doing homelab practice projects, TryHackMe learning paths, and CTF rooms come into play.

- The homelabs are the best way to be able to practice endless scenarios and projects, these can go deeper and get more and more advanced the better I get.
- The TryHackMe learning paths are a structured way to introduce me to the relevant topics, concepts, and tools in a progression that makes sense.
- CTF rooms are fun, and allow me to get more hands on with pentesting tools, to help me better understand practically how an attacker will think about breaking into a system or network.

### Homelab website

And tying this all together will be this website, where I can do writeup logs for these homelab projects, THM rooms, and any other notes I feel like writing along the way.

## The roadmap

So here, the plan can be split into two parts: the certifications and path completions (a checklist of goals), and the ever ongoing homelab project ideas and CTF writeups.
